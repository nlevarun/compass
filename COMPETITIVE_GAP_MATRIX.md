## WHAT THEY DO POORLY (Attack Here)

### 1. No Combined Internal + Public Feedback (90% of market)
**Who:** Productboard (internal only), Canny/UserVoice (public only)

**The Gap:**
- Companies pay for Productboard ($2,400/year) AND Canny ($200/mo) = $4,800/year
- Duplicate work (tag feedback twice, sync manually)
- Inconsistent data (public says one thing, internal says another)

**Compass Advantage:**
- ONE platform, both internal + public
- 2x value, 50% cost ($2,388/year Pro plan)
- Single source of truth

**Attack Vector:**
"Why pay for two tools when Compass does both?"

---

### 2. Revenue-Weighted Prioritization (100% of market)
**Who:** ALL competitors treat votes equally

**The Gap:**
- Free user vote = Enterprise customer vote (makes no sense)
- Manual revenue tagging (requires Salesforce, time-consuming)
- No automatic weighting (PM has to do mental math)

**Compass Advantage:**
- Automatic revenue weighting from CRM data
- Visual indication (not just vote count, but $ impact)
- Privacy-safe (don't expose individual customer revenue)

**Attack Vector:**
"Finally! Prioritize by customer value, not just vote count."

**Example:**
```
Traditional voting:
  Feature A: 100 votes (50 free users, 50 paid)
  Feature B: 50 votes (all $10k/year customers)
  → Build Feature A (more votes)

Revenue-weighted:
  Feature A: 100 votes × $50 avg = $5,000 impact
  Feature B: 50 votes × $10,000 avg = $500,000 impact
  → Build Feature B (100x more revenue)
```

### 2. Self-Hosted Open Source (**+10 points**)
**Market White Space:** Nobody in this category offers self-hosted

**Why It Matters:**

- Enterprises hesitant to send customer feedback to third-party SaaS
- Developers prefer open source (auditable, secure, trustworthy)

**Compass Strategy:**
- MIT license (permissive, commercial-friendly)
- Docker/Kubernetes deployment (easy self-host)
- Full feature parity (not crippled)
- Free forever (only pay hosting costs)

**Attack Vector:**
"Own your data. Self-host for free. No vendor lock-in."

### 3. Real-Time Collaboration (**+6 points**)
**Market White Space:** Nobody has Figma-like collaboration

**Why It Matters:**
- Modern expectation (Figma, Notion, Google Docs set bar)
- Teams want to analyze feedback together (not async)
- Reduces duplicated work (see what teammates are doing)

**Compass Features:**
- Live cursors (see teammates' mouse pointers)
- Presence indicators (who's online, viewing what)
- Real-time comments (instant, not delayed)
- Co-editing (simultaneous note tagging)

**Attack Vector:**
"Analyze feedback as a team in real-time. It's like Figma for PMs."

### 4. Multi-Modal Feedback Analysis (Future)
**Market White Space:** All tools are text-only

**Why It Matters:**
- Voice feedback (sales calls, customer calls, Gong recordings)
- Visual feedback (screenshots, Loom videos, Figma links)
- Missed insights (text captures 50% of feedback nuance)

**Compass Roadmap (Year 2):**
- Audio transcription (Whisper API, integrate Gong/Chorus)
- Image analysis (OCR, visual bug reports)
- Video timestamps (Loom integration, clip-to-feedback)

**Attack Vector:**
"Analyze feedback from voice, video, and images. Not just text."

---

### 5. Predictive Churn from Feedback (Future)
**Market White Space:** Nobody predicts churn proactively

**Why It Matters:**
- Reactive = see negative feedback after customer churns (too late)
- Proactive = predict churn from sentiment trends, engagement drops
- ROI = save $10k customer with $100 intervention

**Compass ML Model (Year 2):**
- Train on historical data (feedback sentiment → churn/retention)
- Alert when customer shows churn signals (3 negative posts, 30 days inactive)
- Suggest interventions (reach out, offer beta access, prioritize request)

**Attack Vector:**
"Predict churn before it happens. Save customers with data, not gut feel."

---

### 6. Feedback-to-Revenue Attribution (Future)
**Market White Space:** Can't measure ROI of building features

**Why It Matters:**
- PMs can't justify investment ("we think this will help retention")
- Execs ask "did that feature increase revenue?" (no way to measure)
- Flywheel: measure impact → justify more resources → build more → measure more

**Compass Analytics (Year 2):**
- Track: Which customers requested Feature X?
- Measure: Did their ARR increase after we shipped it?
- Prove: "Feature X led to $500k expansion revenue in 6 months"

**Attack Vector:**
"Prove the ROI of product decisions. Feedback → Revenue attribution."
